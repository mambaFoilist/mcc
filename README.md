# Discretized Clock Project

**Guiding Philosphy & Goal Outline**

I disagree with the victim mindset. Cancer can take away as many loved ones as it wants, hell, people ask if I have cancer due to my autoimmune conditions. But I spit in its face by caring for those I love and living my life to the fullest. Part of the latter, for me, is teaching myself hardware :P. 

The aim of this project is to constructing a clock that solely uses discrete components and digital logic. It will display 3 times zones: LA, NY, Korea. Initially, I planned to use a 555 timer, yet, given its relatively poor frequency stability in comparison to what I am trying to accomplish. I am opting for a 32.768kHz crystal oscillator. I plan to make this clock as a gift meaning that I hope she will not need to tend too much to the clock. In other words, I am not interested in gifting a "ppm white elephant" :P.
